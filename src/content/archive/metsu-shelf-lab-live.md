On October 29 the Metsu site went to v3, and the hero stopped describing the product and
started running it. Shelf Lab is a retail layout test: you pick layout A or layout B, press
run, and a synthetic crowd of 10,000 shoppers walks the store. The numbers are
**synthetic and illustrative**, not customer data, and the page says so.

## What shipped

- **A live Shelf Lab on [metsu.ai](https://metsu.ai).** Layout A vs B, 10,000 shoppers, in the browser.
- **A six-mode gallery**, each mode with a real capture from the running build.
- **An industry picker** that routes each visitor to the right contact.
- **A 12-page deck** at [metsu.ai/deck/metsu-xr-deck.pdf](https://metsu.ai/deck/metsu-xr-deck.pdf).
- **A headset build** with plain-word controls and a guided tour, verified in a headless browser. Not yet verified inside the Quest, and I will say so until it is.

## Why it matters

Metsu is an XR company for physical industries. The bet is that a store, a truck bay or a
factory floor can be tested as a simulation before anything is moved, so the expensive
mistakes happen on a screen. Shelf Lab is the first product on that bet, and it is the
first one a stranger can open and try.

## What is still open

Real-GPU performance numbers, a Quest-native pass, and the next two products
(a mockup room and a hazard drill) are planned, not built.
