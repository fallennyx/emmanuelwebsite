A browser open-world superhero sandbox built from a single prompt: a procedural city,
switchable powers, crowd NPCs and buildings you can level, with no external assets. Every
mesh, texture and sound is generated in code.

It was an experiment in how far one prompt can take a whole open-world game. It got far
enough to make me take the superpower fantasy seriously, and it is the ancestor of
[the VR game Metsu is building now](/archive/metsu-studios-vr-game/).

The source is private; the write-ups on this site are the public record.
